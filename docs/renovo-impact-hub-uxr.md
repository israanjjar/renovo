## 1. Research Objectives

### 1.1 Primary Questions

1. What motivates individuals to contribute to environmental and social impact platforms?
2. How do users evaluate trust and transparency in donation/subscription platforms?
3. What level of impact visibility do users need to feel their contribution matters?
4. How do different demographics (Gen Z, professionals, corporate) differ in their expectations?

### 1.2 Research Methods

| Method | Purpose | Participants |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| Competitive analysis | Understand existing landscape | Mossy Earth, Re:wild, NCC, Greenly, UN Offset |
| User persona development | Define target segments | 4 archetypes |
| Journey mapping | Identify friction & delight points | End-to-end membership lifecycle |
| Storyboard | Visualize first-time experience | Primary persona |

## 3. User Journey Map

### 3.1 Five Phases of the Member Experience

```
Discovery → Onboarding → Active Membership → Impact Review → Advocacy
```

| Phase | User Actions | Touchpoints | Emotions | Opportunities |
|-------|-------------|-------------|----------|---------------|
| **Discovery** | Sees social post, friend referral, or event | Landing page, social media, word of mouth | Curious, skeptical | Compelling hero section with real impact data |
| **Onboarding** | Signs up, picks tier, selects SDG focus | Registration flow, tier selection, SDG picker | Hopeful, slightly overwhelmed | Progressive disclosure — start simple |
| **Active Membership** | Views dashboard, reads updates, votes on projects | Impact dashboard, monthly reports, community feed | Engaged, connected | Real-time data updates, push notifications |
| **Impact Review** | Receives monthly impact summary, shares results | Email digest, shareable impact card, dashboard | Proud, validated | Shareable visual cards for social media |
| **Advocacy** | Invites friends, upgrades tier, attends events | Referral system, community events, social sharing | Empowered, loyal | Gamified referral rewards |

## 4. Key Moments & Emotional Analysis

### 4.1 Critical Delight Moments

| Moment | Phase | Why It Matters |
|--------|-------|---------------|
| **First dashboard view** | Onboarding | User sees where their money goes — trust is established or broken here |
| **First impact notification** | Active Membership | Tangible proof their contribution did something real |
| **Monthly impact card** | Impact Review | Shareable artifact that validates their decision to subscribe |
| **Community vote result** | Active Membership | Feeling of democratic agency — "I helped decide this" |

### 4.2 Critical Friction Points

| Friction | Phase | Risk | Mitigation |
|----------|-------|------|-----------|
| "Is this legit?" skepticism | Discovery | Bounce before signup | Transparency dashboard visible pre-signup |
| SDG jargon overload | Onboarding | Confusion, drop-off | Plain language first, SDG badges as secondary labels |
| No immediate feedback | Active Membership | "Did my money do anything?" | Real-time progress indicators, weekly micro-updates |
| Renewal decision | Impact Review | Churn | Pre-renewal impact summary showing cumulative contribution |

## 5. Competitive UX Analysis

### 5.1 Competitor Comparison Matrix

| Feature | Mossy Earth | Re:wild | NCC | Greenly | **Renovo (Target)** |
|---------|-------------|---------|-----|---------|-------------------|
| Subscription model | Yes ($9/mo) | Donations | Donations | B2B tiers | Yes (3 tiers) |
| Impact dashboard | Basic | None | Annual report | Carbon dashboard | **Real-time, per-user** |
| SDG alignment | No | Partial | No | Carbon only | **Full SDG mapping** |
| Financial transparency | 80% to impact | Unclear | Annual report | N/A | **Dollar-for-dollar breakdown** |
| Community voting | No | No | No | No | **Yes — monthly project votes** |
| Social sharing | Basic | No | No | Badges | **Monthly impact cards** |
| Dual impact (env + social) | Env only | Env only | Env only | Carbon only | **Both env & social** |
| Mobile experience | App | Responsive | Responsive | Dashboard | **Mobile-first responsive** |

### 5.2 Key Differentiator: Transparency

Mossy Earth leads competitors with their transparency dashboard (80% to impact), but no competitor offers:
- Per-dollar allocation breakdown visible to users
- Community-driven project selection
- Dual environmental + social impact tracking
- SDG-aligned metrics at the individual contributor level

## 6. Design Principles

### Principle 1: Transparency First
Every dollar has a visible destination. Users should never wonder where their money went. Show the financial breakdown on every project page and in every monthly report.

### Principle 2: Impact Made Tangible
Abstract numbers mean nothing. Translate metrics into concrete visuals: "12 trees planted" > "0.2 tonnes CO₂ offset" > raw data tables. Use satellite imagery, photos, and progress bars.

### Principle 3: Progressive Complexity
Start with a simple dashboard. Let power users (Marcus, Sarah) drill into detailed SDG metrics and ESG reports. Don't overwhelm Anika on day one.

### Principle 4: Community Agency
Members vote, members decide, members see results. The platform is not a donation box — it's a collective decision-making tool.

### Principle 5: Shareable by Default
Every impact milestone should produce a shareable artifact (image card, link, social post template). Viral growth comes from members proudly sharing their impact.

### Principle 6: Mobile-First, Dashboard-Rich
Gen Z and professionals check their phones first. The mobile experience must be complete, not a stripped-down version. Desktop adds density, not features.

## 9. Open Research Questions

1. **Pricing sensitivity**: What is the maximum Gen Z users will pay monthly? ($10? $15? $20?)
2. **Geographic scope**: Should Phase 1 focus exclusively on BC projects for local resonance, or go national?
3. **Voting mechanics**: Should voting be weighted by tier (VIP gets more votes) or one-member-one-vote?
4. **Impact card format**: Do users prefer static image cards or animated/interactive share links?
5. **Notification frequency**: Weekly updates vs. milestone-only — which drives engagement without causing fatigue?

---

## 10. Recommendations for PRD

Based on this research, the PRD should prioritize:

1. **Landing page with transparency preview** — visible pre-signup to overcome skepticism
2. **Three-tier subscription flow** — simple onboarding with SDG focus selection
3. **Impact dashboard** — real-time project tracking with dollar-for-dollar breakdown
4. **Monthly impact card generation** — shareable social media artifact
5. **Community voting system** — monthly project selection by members
6. **Mobile-first responsive design** — complete experience on all devices

The MVP (Phase 1) should focus on environmental projects only (SDGs 7, 12, 13, 14, 15) with 1–3 initial projects, expanding to social impact in Phase 2.
